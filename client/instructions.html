Examination Instructions

  1. Read every question carefully before selecting or writing your answer.
  2. Do not refresh or reload the page during the examination.
  3. Do not close the browser window until the exam is completed.
  4. The examination timer starts immediately and cannot be paused.
  5. Your answers are automatically saved while attempting the exam.
  6. Use the Save & Next button to move to the next question.
  7. You may return to previously visited questions before final submission.
  8. Use Mark for Review if you wish to revisit a question later.
  9. The examination will be submitted automatically when the timer reaches zero.
  10. Please ensure you have a stable internet connection throughout the examination.
